Smeargle Race Rules:
1. Only Smeargle in battle
2. No items in battle
3. No glitches, exploits, or rng manips in the overworld
4. No evasion boosting moves


Rule Clarifications:

You cannot use Pokeballs for any purpose other than catching HM users

You are allowed you skip Marowak with a Pokedoll

The bike skip glitch is banned

All in-battle battle-related glitches are allowed (badge-boost, Fly-paralysis glitch, gen1 miss, etc)

Turbo-fire / Rapid-fire keys are allowed (and highly encouraged)

Blackouts count as resets

You must use Livesplit to track real-time.

It is strictly against the rules to modify any property using GameHook

Save states are banned (they can be used for testing purposes only)

You must play on 4x game-speed
