Graphics Toggles:
Trainer Teams:
Displays the enemy trainer's team while battling them. This option must be enabled for the following option "All Trainers" to work.

All Trainers:
When disabled the overlay will only display the teams of key trainers (Rivals, Gym Leaders, E4, Champion).

Show Trainer Illustrations:
When enabled this will display illustrations of the trainer under their team when you fight them.

Show Wild Pokemon:
Displays wild Pokemon in the same way as trainer Pokemon when this option is checked.

Show screens, accruacy, and evasion:
The overlay will display pop ups when your Pokemon is behind a screen or has had its accuracy or evasion modified.


Calculation Toggles:
Show Guarenteed Crits in Effective Power:
If the Pokemon is guarenteed a critical hit using a high-crit-rate move the overlay will calculate in the crit multiplier when displaying effective power. High-crit-rate moves are: Crabhammer, Karate Chop, Slash, Razor Leaf.

Rock Tunnel Darkness:
